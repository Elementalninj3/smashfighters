1. CONTROLS

  Jump          W / Up / Space
  Attack        J / Z
  Special       K / X
  Shield        L / C

2. CORE DESIGN
===============================================================================

This is a 1v1 Super Smash Bros.-style platform fighter. Characters are balls
(skins from public/assets/). The key principle:

  - Attacking does NOT launch your body (unlike a "body-ram" system).
  - The ball stays roughly in place and emits a strike hitbox around itself.
  - Movement and attacking are SEPARATE input layers.
  - Attacks have 3 phases: WINDUP -> ACTIVE -> ENDLAG (like Smash).
  - During windup you can be hit (whiff punish window).
  - During endlag you are vulnerable before you can act again.

The exception is DASH ATTACK, which commits the body forward briefly.


3. ATTACK INPUTS (4-directional)
===============================================================================

  Attack + Up      =  Tilt Up  (grounded)  /  Up Air   (airborne)
  Attack + Down    =  Tilt Down (grounded) /  Down Air (airborne)
  Attack + Left    =  Tilt Back or Tilt Forward (depends on facing)
  Attack + Right   =  Tilt Forward or Tilt Back (depends on facing)
  Attack (no dir)  =  Jab (grounded) / Neutral Air (airborne)

  Hold Attack 0.14s+ = SMASH variant (same direction, heavier)
  Attack while running forward = Dash Attack

6. KNOCKBACK AND DAMAGE
===============================================================================

  Damage accumulates as a percentage (like Smash). Higher % = more knockback.

  Knockback formula:
    kb = (damage * 7 + (baseKnockback + knockbackScaling * defenderPercent))
         * (1 + defenderPercent / 80) * koPower / defenderWeight

  - Knockback angle determines launch direction (0 = horizontal, pi/2 = up).
  - Angle flips if defender is on the opposite side of the attacker.
  - Hitstun scales with percent: hitstun * (1 + damagePercent / 150).
  - Weight (per-character, 0.85-1.25) reduces knockback for heavier characters.

  Hit pause: on hit, the game freezes briefly (0.03-0.11s) proportional to
  knockback for game feel. Higher KB = longer freeze.


7. DEFENSIVE MECHANICS
===============================================================================

SHIELD (hold L/C / Numpad3):
  - Grounded only. Blocks incoming attacks.
  - Shield HP drains at 80/sec while held, regens at 40/sec when released.
  - Shield break = 0.8s stun.
  - Cannot move while shielding.

DODGE / ROLL (Shift / Numpad0):
  Ground dodge:
    - Forward roll with 0.25s invulnerability.
    - Dodge cooldown: 0.4s.
    - Velocity damped to 30% at end.

  Air dodge:
    - 0.3s invulnerability, directional.
    - If angled down + near ground = WAVEDASH:
      - 0.1s invuln, 520px/s horizontal burst, snaps to ground.
      - Cooldown: 0.6s.

  Air dodge passing through fighters (dodging = true skips body collision).

TECH (press shield during hitstun):
  - Set a tech window (0.18s) while being hit.
  - If you hit the ground while stunned and tech window is active:
    - Bounce up at 160 units.
    - 50% hitstun reduction.
    - Tech animation: 0.1s.
  - Tech particles spawn at impact point.

DI (Directional Influence):
  - Hold a direction during hitstun to influence knockback angle.
  - Shifts knockback angle by up to 18 degrees.
  - Survival DI: hold toward center of stage to reduce blast zone risk.


8. MOVEMENT
===============================================================================

Ground movement:
  Run speed:   154 px/s (varies per character, range ~119-196)
  Acceleration: 1400 px/s^2
  Deceleration: 1260 px/s^2

Air movement:
  Air speed:    126 px/s (varies per character, range ~103-150)
  Air accel:    980 px/s^2
  Air drag:     0.97 per frame (gradual slowdown)

Physics:
  Gravity:       2200 px/s^2
  Max fall speed: 1200 px/s
  Fast fall:     1.8x gravity (hold down while falling past apex)

Jump:
  Full hop:     680 velocity (varies per character, range 620-760)
  Shorthop:     420 velocity (hold down while jumping)
  Double jump:  580 velocity (1 air jump, reset on landing)
  Jump cut:     multiply vy by 0.4 on early release (short hop pressure)

Lunge system (attack movement):
  Lunges are short committed steps that decay per frame:
    Grip:  0.86 per frame (friction)
    Break: ignored below 150 velocity
  This gives attacks a visible forward commit without free flight.


9. STAGE
===============================================================================

Layout ("Battlefield"):
  - Main ground: centered, 65% of canvas width, at 78% height.
  - One floating platform: center, 14% width, 140px above ground.
  - Floating platform bobs gently (sin wave, 3px amplitude).
  - Drop-through platforms: hold Down to fall through.
  - Platform tolerance: 8px vertical snap window (fixed from old vy-inflated bug).

Blast zones:
  - 300px beyond visible stage on all sides.
  - Top zone 1.5x margin (450px).
  - Crossing a blast zone = KO (lose a stock).

Ledge:
  - Grab range: 42px horizontal, 26px vertical from ground edge.
  - 0.8s invulnerability on grab.
  - Ledge options:
      Jump (W/Up)       - inward hop, 600 up, 300 horizontal
      Get-up (Up)       - low commit, 260 up, 160 horizontal, 0.35s invuln
      Drop (Down)       - fall away from stage
      Attack (J)        - inward attack, 200 horizontal
      Stay (no input)   - remain on ledge

Wall tech:
  - Tech window: 0.12s (press shield before hitting ground).
  - Bounce: -200 velocity, 50% stun reduction.
  - Tech particles: green sparkles.


10. MATCH RULES
===============================================================================

  - 1v1 stock battle.
  - Default: 3 stocks each.
  - KO = lose a stock, respawn at center after 2s.
  - 1.5s spawn invulnerability.
  - Game over when one player has 0 stocks.


11. KNOWN ISSUES
===============================================================================

  - Player 2's "down" and "special" both map to Numpad2.
    Pressing special always registers as "down" too.
    Fix: rebind one of them (e.g., down = Numpad0, or special = NumpadPeriod).

  - Smash charge doesn't visually show charge level (no glow/animation).

  - No off-stage ledge grab for floating platform (only main ground has ledges).

  - The `getCardinalDir` uses 'jump' key for up-direction, which means pressing
    jump during an attack charge starts charging rather than jumping.
    This is by design (Smash uses the same stick for jump and up-tilt),
    but may feel unintuitive on keyboard.
